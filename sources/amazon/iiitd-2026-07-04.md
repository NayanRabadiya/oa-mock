Transcribed from a short note (`mock_amazon.txt`, not kept in this repo). Only the two coding statements were shared.

## 1. Rearrangements with short even runs

You are given an array `A` of distinct positive integers of length `n`. Determine the number of ways to rearrange the elements of the array such that the maximum length of any contiguous subarray consisting entirely of even numbers is at most 2. Since the answer can be very large, output it modulo 10⁹ + 7.

→ Mock question: `data/amazon/questions/evenrun.json` (*Even Blocks of At Most Two*).

## 2. Palindrome-rearrangeable subsequences

You are given a string `s` of length `n`, consisting of lowercase English letters. Determine the number of subsequences of `s` such that the subsequence can be rearranged into a palindrome. The empty subsequence is also considered palindromic.

→ Mock question: `data/amazon/questions/palsub.json` (*Palindrome-Ready Subsequences*). The mock counts subsequences by the positions chosen and takes the answer modulo 10⁹ + 7. The note didn't say either, so these are the mock's assumptions.

Neither statement included constraints. The mock uses `1 ≤ n ≤ 2 × 10⁵`.
